Nicole presented her work focused on interpreting variants of unknown significance for ornithine transcarbamylase (OTC) deficiency.  This work used an e.coli-based expression vector and RNA studies using an OTC minigene. 

Nicole also present this work at the [Society for Inherited Metabolic Disorders](https://simd.org/) meeting in May 2026 and at the Urea Cycle Consortium Meeting in July 2026.
